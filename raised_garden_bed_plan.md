# Elevated Garden Planter — Build Plan (Rectangle, Leg-Style)

**Assumption:** 4 standard 8-ft (96") 2x4s (actual size 1.5" x 3.5"). Footprint: **36" x 18"**, same 2:1 ratio as the reference photo, standing **30" tall** on 4 continuous corner legs, with a 7" deep box on top. This fits your 4 boards with 30" of scrap to spare.

## Tools Needed
- Circular saw
- Drill/driver (1/8" pilot bit + driver bit)
- Tape measure, speed square, pencil, clamps
- Staple gun (for the bottom liner)

## Shopping List (not covered by your 4 2x4s)
- ~40 exterior/deck screws — 3" for box-to-leg joints, 1.5" for the bottom cleats
- Galvanized hardware cloth OR heavy landscape fabric, cut to ~36"x18" (this box is elevated, so it needs an actual bottom to hold soil in)
- Optional: exterior wood glue at the joints for extra rigidity since this one gets moved around

## Cut List (14 pieces)

| Piece | Qty | Length | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Legs | 4 | 30" | Corner posts, run continuous from ground to top of box |
| Long slats (front/back) | 4 | 36" | Box walls, full outer length (2 per course x 2 courses) |
| Short slats (left/right) | 4 | 15" | Box walls, fit between legs on the short ends (2 per course x 2 courses) |
| Bottom cleats | 2 | 15" | Screwed to inside faces of legs to support the liner |

**Box depth:** 2 courses stacked = 7" of growing depth. Legs give ~23" of ground clearance below the box.

## Board-by-Board Cut Map

| Board | Cuts | Used | Scrap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Board 1 (96") | 3 legs @ 30" | 90" | 6" |
| Board 2 (96") | 1 leg @ 30" + 1 long slat @ 36" + 2 short slats @ 15" | 96" | 0" |
| Board 3 (96") | 2 long slats @ 36" + 1 short slat @ 15" | 87" | 9" |
| Board 4 (96") | 1 long slat @ 36" + 3 short slats @ 15" (2 for box, 1 spare toward the 2nd cleat — see note) | 81" | 15" |

Note: this map yields 4 legs, 4 long slats, and 6 short-length pieces total (4 for the box + 2 cleats) — exactly what's needed. Total scrap ≈ 30", plenty of buffer for kerf loss and a mis-cut or two.

## Assembly

1. **Cut all 14 pieces.** Label with painter's tape (LEG, LONG36, SHORT15) so nothing gets mixed up.
2. **Pilot-drill** both ends of every LONG36 and SHORT15 slat (2 holes per end) to stop splitting when screwing into end grain.
3. **Stand up the 4 legs** and clamp them plumb in a 36"x18" rectangle — measure both diagonals to confirm square (they should match).
4. **Attach the bottom course.** About 23" up from the leg bottoms (leaving that much ground clearance), screw the 2 long slats (36") to the outside faces of the legs first — 2 screws per corner. Then screw the 2 short slats (15") between the legs, butted against the inside faces of the long slats — 2 screws per corner.
5. **Attach the top course** directly on top, same order (long slats first, then short), flush with the top of the legs.
6. **Install the bottom cleats.** Screw the 2 cleats (15") horizontally between the legs, just below the bottom course, forming a ledge for the liner.
7. **Add the liner.** Cut hardware cloth or landscape fabric to fit the 36"x18" interior, drape it over the cleats, staple to the inside faces to hold it in place. Lets water drain, keeps soil in.
8. **Check square and level**, then fill with soil.

**Estimated time:** 2–2.5 hours.
**Difficulty:** Beginner-to-intermediate — same joinery as the square version, just longer spans on two sides.
