# Shop Tote / Divided Storage Bin

A classic open-top wood tote with a center carry handle that doubles as a divider — splits into two compartments, easy to grab and move, built entirely from what's already in the shop.

**Overall size:** 20" L x 12" W x 8" H
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Build time:** ~3-4 hours, one sitting (no glue-dry gaps required if you screw everything, though letting glue set overnight before hard use is smart)

---

## Cut List

| Piece | Material | Qty | Dimensions | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bottom | 3/4" white plywood | 1 | 20" x 12" | Full footprint |
| Side walls | 1/2" plywood | 2 | 20" x 8" | Long walls, sit on outer edges of bottom |
| End walls | 3/4" white plywood | 2 | 11" x 8" | Capped between the two side walls |
| Handle/divider bar | 2x4 (actual 1.5" x 3.5") | 1 | 11" long | Ripped down if you want it lighter — see note below |

**From your 2x4 (8 ft):** you only need 11" of it. Save the rest — plenty left for the next project.

**Ripping the handle bar:** a full 1.5" x 3.5" 2x4 works fine as-is for the handle — sturdy, easy to grip. If you want it less chunky, rip it down the middle lengthwise with a straightedge and circular saw to get a 1.5" x 1.75" bar. Not required, just a look/weight preference.

---

## Wood Notes
Everything's already on hand — no lumber to buy. The white 3/4" ply as ends/bottom gives you the load-bearing structure and a clean look; 1/2" ply is plenty for the side walls since they're not carrying weight, just containing it.

---

## Tools Needed
- Circular saw + straightedge/track guide (rip and crosscut everything)
- Clamped stop block (for repeat crosscuts — you need two identical end walls and two identical side walls, so set a stop block once you've got your first piece cut to length and bang out the rest against it)
- Drill/driver
- Wood glue
- Clamps
- Tape measure, speed square
- Sandpaper (round over the handle bar and top edges so nothing bites your hand)

No jigsaw needed — the handle is screwed to the inside faces, not passed through a cutout hole, so there's no curved or plunge cutting anywhere in this build.

---

## Build Instructions

1. **Cut the bottom.** From the 3/4" white ply, cut one piece 20" x 12".

2. **Cut the side walls.** From the 1/2" ply, cut two pieces 20" x 8". Cut the first, then clamp a stop block at 20" and cut the second against it so both match exactly.

3. **Cut the end walls.** From the 3/4" white ply, cut two pieces 11" x 8". Same stop-block method for a matching pair.

4. **Cut the handle bar.** From the 2x4, cut one piece 11" long.

5. **Assemble the box shell.**
   - Run a bead of wood glue along the bottom edges of both side walls. Stand them up on the long edges of the bottom panel, flush to the outer edge. Clamp.
   - Drill pilot holes (about 1/8") through the bottom into the side wall edges, roughly every 4", then drive 1-1/4" wood screws up through the bottom into the sides.
   - Glue and set the two end walls in place between the side walls, flush to the ends of the bottom. Clamp.
   - Pilot-drill and screw through the side walls into the end wall edges (2-3 screws per corner), and through the bottom into the end walls.

6. **Mount the handle bar.** Measure and mark 10" in from each end wall (the horizontal midpoint) on the inside faces of both side walls. Position the handle bar so its top sits about 1" below the top edge of the side walls — this leaves clearance to grip it and keeps it from being the first thing that snags when you're loading the bin. Glue the ends of the bar against the inside faces, clamp, then pilot-drill and screw through the outside of each side wall into the ends of the handle bar (2 screws per side).

7. **Sand everything.** Round over all top edges and the handle bar especially — this is what your hand touches every time you carry it.

8. **Finish (optional).** Bare is fine for shop use. If it's living somewhere visible, a coat of wipe-on poly or your usual finish will do it — you've already got that stocked.
